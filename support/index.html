Frequently Asked Questions

When will FodyPay launch?

FodyPay is currently in development. Join the waitlist on our homepage and you’ll be among the first to know when we open access.

What countries will FodyPay support?

FodyPay is designed for global use — we’re targeting support for 150+ countries at launch. Specific availability will depend on local regulatory requirements.

Who issues the FodyPay virtual card?

Virtual cards are issued by Celtic Bank, Member FDIC, through Stripe’s card-issuing program. FodyPay is a technology platform, not a bank.

How do I request a refund?

Email support@fodypay.com within 14 days of the transaction in question. Include your account email and a description of the issue. See our Terms of Service for the full refund policy.

How do I report a security issue or unauthorized transaction?

Email security@fodypay.com immediately. For suspected account compromise, include your account email and a description of the activity. We treat all security reports as high priority.

How do I close my account or request data deletion?

Email support@fodypay.com with the subject line “Account Closure Request” or “Data Deletion Request”. See our Privacy Policy for details on data retention obligations.

I have a legal or compliance question.

Email legal@fodypay.com. For law enforcement requests, please include your jurisdiction, the relevant legal authority, and contact information for your agency.
